RoleReady AI is a student project built as part of an IBM SkillsBuild internship training program. The idea is grounded in UN Sustainable Development Goal 8 — Decent Work and Economic Growth, which identifies skill-job mismatch as a barrier to youth employment worldwide.

The app shows you the gap between your current skills and a sample role template, gives you a prioritised study plan, and links you to free learning resources. It doesn't predict hiring chances — that's not something a checklist can do. The real work still happens on your end.
